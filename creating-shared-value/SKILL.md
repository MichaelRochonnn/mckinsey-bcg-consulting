# Creating Shared Value (CSV)

Use this skill when you need to design or evaluate business initiatives that simultaneously advance economic and social goals. This framework moves beyond traditional trade-offs between profit and purpose.

## When to Use

- Defining or refining corporate purpose
- Addressing social issues through business operations
- Designing new business models
- Evaluating or transforming CSR initiatives
- Seeking competitive advantage through social innovation
- Aligning ESG strategies with core business operations

## Framework Overview

The CSV framework uses a 2x2 matrix with two axes:
- **Economic Value**: Profitability and financial return
- **Social Value**: Contribution to society and community well-being

The goal is to identify and design initiatives that achieve BOTH values simultaneously (the "AND" not "OR" logic).

## Procedure

### 1. Map Current Activities

Construct the 2x2 matrix and categorize existing initiatives:
- **Bottom-Right (PPP)**: Pure economic profit, low social value (Traditional Capitalism)
- **Top-Left (CSR)**: Pure social value, low economic return (Philanthropy)
- **Bottom-Left**: Low economic and social value (Activities to eliminate)

### 2. Target the Top-Right Quadrant

Identify the CSV zone (Top-Right):
- High economic value AND high social value
- Policies and operating practices that enhance competitiveness
- Simultaneously advancing economic and social conditions
- This is the target state for strategic initiatives

### 3. Design CSV Initiatives

Develop initiatives that follow the CSV logic:
- Ask: "How can we solve social problems in ways that also strengthen our business?"
- Look for intersections between business needs and societal challenges
- Examples: Supply chain development, health and wellness programs, environmental efficiency

### 4. Validate Alignment

Ensure initiatives meet CSV criteria:
- Does it advance company competitiveness?
- Does it create measurable social progress?
- Is it integrated into core operations, not just philanthropy?
- Does it create sustainable value (not one-off)?

## Key Principles

- **Logic is "AND" not "OR"**: Economic value AND social value, not trade-offs
- **Not Philanthropy**: CSV is about core business, not charitable giving
- **Strategic Integration**: Social impact must strengthen the business model
- **Measurable**: Both economic and social outcomes should be quantifiable

## Output

Generate strategic directions and business initiatives that create mutual benefit for the company and society.
