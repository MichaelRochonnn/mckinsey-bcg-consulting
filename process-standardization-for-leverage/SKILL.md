# Process Standardization for Leverage

## When to Use This Skill
Use this skill when:
- Preparing to delegate work to team members or external partners
- Tasks currently depend on individual expertise or "craftsmanship"
- Need to scale operations beyond individual capacity
- Planning to outsource functions to external vendors
- Want to integrate external resources into your workflow

## Core Workflow

### 1. Internal Delegation (Converting Craftsmanship to Procedures)

**Identify craftsmanship tasks:**
- Locate tasks that only specific individuals can perform
- Recognize tacit knowledge that isn't documented

**Study and refine:**
- Thoroughly analyze the task execution
- Identify optimal methods and best practices
- Refine the process to achieve consistent, high-quality results

**Convert to reproducible procedure:**
- Document each step clearly
- Create manuals or standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- Ensure the procedure can be followed by others without the original expert

**Result:** Enables work standardization and sharing, allowing others to perform the task effectively.

### 2. External Outsourcing (Standardizing Interfaces)

**Standardize the interface:**
- Define clear input/output specifications
- Establish communication protocols
- Document integration points with your business processes

**Standardize the business:**
- Prepare internal processes to absorb external "craftsmanship"
- Create systems to integrate external expertise into your organization
- Ensure compatibility between internal and external workflows

### 3. Leverage IT Tools

**Utilize standardized interfaces:**
- Implement IT tools that work with standardized processes
- Leverage automation where possible
- Use open interfaces to access external assets and services

**Boost productivity:**
- Integrate external resources seamlessly
- Scale operations through technology
- Reduce manual coordination overhead

### 4. Goal: Toyota Production System Approach

**Internal wisdom sharing:**
- Capture on-site knowledge and make it accessible
- Distribute expertise across the organization

**External wisdom absorption:**
- Standardize to incorporate external best practices
- Continuously improve through external integration

## Key Considerations

### Prerequisites
- Clear desire to delegate or outsource
- Existence of tacit knowledge or craftsmanship worth capturing

### Constraints
- Requires upfront time investment to document processes
- May face resistance from individuals holding tacit knowledge
- Needs commitment to maintaining and updating standards

### Success Factors
- Thorough documentation of procedures
- Clear interface specifications
- Ongoing refinement of standards
- Management support for standardization efforts
