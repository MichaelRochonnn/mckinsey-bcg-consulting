# Left and Right Brain Connectivity

## When to Use This Skill
Apply this skill when:
- Aiming for 'Super First-Class' consulting performance or breakthrough innovation
- Current approaches rely too heavily on pure logic and analysis
- Team composition lacks cognitive diversity
- You need to combine rigorous analysis with creative synthesis
- Traditional consulting frameworks feel insufficient for complex challenges

## Prerequisites
- Recognition of individual cognitive biases
- Access to diverse talent pools
- Awareness of your own cognitive tendencies (left-brain vs right-brain dominant)

## Core Concepts

### Left Brain Capability
- Logical analysis
- Engineering rigor
- Structured thinking
- Data-driven decision making

### Right Brain Capability
- Creativity and imagination
- Intuition and pattern recognition
- Sensitivity and emotional intelligence
- Hypothesis generation and synthesis

## Execution Procedure

### 1. Assess Current Cognitive Balance
Recognize that typical top consultants often suffer from 'Left Brain Hypertrophy'—an over-reliance on logic, engineering backgrounds, and structured analysis. Evaluate whether you or your team leans too heavily on one hemisphere.

### 2. Identify the Ohmae Standard
The ideal consultant (like Kenichi Ohmae) possesses:
- PhD-level engineering background (Left Brain strength)
- Exceptional Right Brain creativity and intuition

Use this standard to evaluate individual capabilities and identify gaps.

### 3. Implement Neural Connectivity
Actively connect left and right neuron synapses to enable:
- Logical analysis to inform creative intuition
- Creative hypotheses to guide analytical focus
- Continuous interaction between structured thinking and imaginative synthesis

### 4. Team Composition Strategy
If an individual cannot achieve this balance, construct a team that:
- Includes 'Left Brain' types: logical, rigorous, analytical
- Includes 'Right Brain' types: creative, intuitive, sensitive
- Maintains equilibrium through deliberate cognitive diversity

### 5. Apply Balanced Thinking
Use each hemisphere appropriately:
- **Left Brain**: Rigorous analysis, data validation, structured problem-solving
- **Right Brain**: Creative synthesis, hypothesis generation, intuitive leaps, stakeholder empathy

## Expected Outcome
Enhanced consulting ability combining rigorous logic with creative innovation, enabling 'Super First-Class' performance that exceeds traditional consulting approaches.

## Common Constraints
- Most traditional consultants (e.g., at McKinsey) tend to be 'Left Brain' types
- Relying solely on one hemisphere limits effectiveness
- Achieving individual balance takes time and practice
- Team-based balance requires intentional composition and facilitation
