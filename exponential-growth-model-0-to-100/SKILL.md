# Exponential Growth Model (0 to 100)

This skill guides you through the three critical stages of business scaling: creation (0→1), validation (1→10), and scaling (10→100). The goal is to achieve exponential growth (10x, 100x) rather than linear growth (1.1x).


## When to Use This Skill

- Scaling a validated business idea
- Planning corporate venturing initiatives
- Aiming for Exponential Organization (ExO) status
- Preparing for explosive market expansion
- Transitioning between growth stages


## Prerequisites

- A validated business idea
- Resources for scaling
- Understanding that different skills are required at each stage


## Stage 0→1: Creation/Deepening

**Objective**: Create new business ideas by identifying unmet market needs.

### Procedure

1. **Identify Market Pains**
   - Look for "No" signals in the market
   - Find unmet needs and customer frustrations
   - Recognize that ideas are not scarce—execution is

2. **Generate Ideas Through Competition**
   - Run internal competitions (e.g., "New RING")
   - Encourage diverse perspectives
   - Focus on solving identified pains

3. **Validate Core Concept**
   - Ensure the idea addresses a genuine need
   - Assess feasibility and resource requirements
   - Prepare for transition to validation phase


## Stage 1→10: Validation/Extending

**Objective**: Grow the idea into a niche business through rapid validation.

### Procedure

1. **Establish Revenue Model Hypothesis**
   - Set a convincing and testable revenue model
   - Define clear monetization pathways
   - Ensure economic viability

2. **Define Value KPIs**
   - Identify metrics directly linked to customer value
   - Establish measurement systems
   - Focus on leading indicators, not just lagging ones

3. **Execute Rapid PDCA Cycles**
   - Plan: Design experiments to test hypotheses
   - Do: Implement quickly and gather data
   - Check: Analyze results against expectations
   - Act: Pivot, persevere, or iterate based on findings

4. **Find the Winning Path**
   - Identify the most promising growth trajectory
   - Validate product-market fit
   - Prepare for scaling phase

**Note**: This is the "Lean Startup" phase—speed and learning are critical.


## Stage 10→100: Scaling/Newing

**Objective**: Achieve explosive expansion to become an industry standard.

### Procedure

1. **Patternization (Paperization)**
   - Convert tacit knowledge to explicit documentation
   - Create standardized manuals and processes
   - Enable knowledge sharing across the organization
   - Ensure consistency at scale

2. **Accumulate S-Curves**
   - Monitor current S-curve saturation
   - Identify new demand before current curve peaks
   - Develop "Option Value" by preparing next growth curves
   - Transition smoothly between growth phases

3. **Scale Operations**
   - Build infrastructure for exponential growth
   - Hire and train for scale
   - Implement systems that support 10x expansion


## Exponential Organization (ExO) Traits

To achieve true exponential growth, incorporate these ExO characteristics:

### Massive Transformative Purpose (MTP)
- Define a huge, non-linear goal
- Align the organization around a transformative vision
- Inspire stakeholders and attract talent

### SCALE IDEAS
Implement attributes that enable 10x growth:
- **S**taff on Demand
- **C**ommunity & Crowd
- **A**lgorithms
- **L**everaged Assets
- **E**ngagement
- **I**nterfaces
- **D**ashboards
- **E**xperiments
- **A**utonomy
- **S**ocial Technologies


## Common Pitfalls

- **Failing to transition**: Each stage requires different skills—stagnation occurs if you don't adapt
- **Linear thinking**: Aim for 10x growth, not 1.1x improvements
- **Premature scaling**: Don't skip validation—scale only after finding the winning path
- **Ignoring S-curve limits**: Always prepare the next growth curve before saturation


## Output

A market-leading business that has grown from an idea to an industry standard through structured scaling phases, positioned for exponential growth.
