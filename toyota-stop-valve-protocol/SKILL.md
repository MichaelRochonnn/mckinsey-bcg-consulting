# Toyota Stop Valve Protocol

This protocol governs the response to abnormalities in production, emphasizing immediate stopping, root cause analysis, and cultural support for the reporting employee.

## When to Use

Use this protocol immediately upon detection of **any** of the following:

- A visual abnormality or defect on the production line
- A deviation from the defined standard
- A malfunction in equipment or process

## Execution Steps

1. **Immediate Action**
   Upon discovering any abnormality, immediately stop the production line by pulling the 'stop valve'.

2. **Containment**
   Absolutely do not pass the defective item to the next process. Contain the issue at the source.

3. **Root Cause Analysis**
   Ask '5 Whys' to thoroughly investigate the true cause of the problem. Do not rely on surface-level explanations.

4. **Resolution**
   Do not restart the line until the root cause is identified and resolved. Ensure the fix prevents recurrence.

5. **Leadership Response**
   Leadership must **NOT** punish the employee who stopped the line. Instead, express gratitude (e.g., bowing and thanking the employee) for exposing the problem and protecting the company from larger failures.

6. **Cultural Mindset**
   View failure not as a negative, but as a 'precious learning opportunity' and a chance to improve the system.

## Prerequisites

- A defined production process
- Leadership willing to support stops (this protocol fails in cultures where stopping production is a punishable offense)
