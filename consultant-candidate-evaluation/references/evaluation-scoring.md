# Evaluation Scoring Guide

## Scoring Rubric

### Left Brain (1-5 scale)
- **1**: Cannot structure problems logically
- **2**: Basic logical structure, many gaps
- **3**: Adequate logical reasoning, standard approach
- **4**: Strong logical framework, clear thinking
- **5**: Exceptional analytical ability, elegant structuring

### Right Brain (1-5 scale)
- **1**: No pattern recognition, purely linear thinking
- **2**: Limited pattern matching, needs explicit guidance
- **3**: Can recognize obvious patterns, some creativity
- **4**: Strong intuition, creative problem framing
- **5**: Exceptional pattern recognition, innovative insights

### First Experience (1-5 scale)
- **1**: No significant transformative experiences
- **2**: Minor life changes, limited self-reflection
- **3**: Some meaningful experiences, moderate self-awareness
- **4**: Significant transformative experiences, strong introspection
- **5**: Profound life experiences, exceptional self-awareness

## Hiring Thresholds

### Minimum Requirements
- Left Brain: 3 or higher
- Right Brain: 3 or higher
- First Experience: 3 or higher

### Strong Hire Profile
- Left Brain: 4+
- Right Brain: 4+
- First Experience: 4+

### Automatic Rejection
- Any red flag present (Consultant Mutant, Law Graduate without exceptions)
- Right Brain score of 1 or 2 (cannot be trained)
- First Experience score of 1 or 2 (indicates lack of depth)

## Weighting Considerations

- **Right Brain** carries highest weight (hardest to develop)
- **First Experience** is second priority (indicates long-term potential)
- **Left Brain** is baseline requirement (most candidates meet this)
