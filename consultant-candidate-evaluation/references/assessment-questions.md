# Assessment Questions by Criterion

## Left Brain (Logical Thinking)

### Sample Questions
- "Walk me through how you would approach [complex business problem]"
- "What data would you need to solve this problem and how would you analyze it?"
- "Break down this issue into its component parts"

### What to Look For
- Structured, step-by-step reasoning
- Clear articulation of assumptions
- Logical flow from problem to solution

## Right Brain (Pattern Recognition)

### Sample Questions
- "What does this situation remind you of?"
- "Have you seen a similar problem before? How was it solved?"
- "What's your gut instinct about the root cause here?"

### What to Look For
- Quick identification of patterns
- Analogical reasoning across domains
- Creative framing of problems
- Intuitive insights backed by experience

## First Experience (Curiosity)

### Sample Questions
- "Tell me about a time when your perspective fundamentally changed"
- "What experience made you question who you are?"
- "When have you had to redefine yourself in a new environment?"

### What to Look For
- Genuine curiosity about the world
- Ability to reflect on personal growth
- Experiences that challenged assumptions
- Evidence of self-awareness and introspection

## First Experience Examples

### Overseas Living
- Living abroad for 1+ years, not just short trips
- Navigating cultural differences that forced adaptation
- Learning to communicate across language barriers

### NPO/Volunteer Work
- Direct exposure to hardship or poverty
- Working with populations vastly different from their background
- Experiences that highlighted privilege and inequality

### Deep Introspection Periods
- Periods of social withdrawal or isolation
- Times of questioning career or life direction
- Experiences that required rebuilding identity

### Other Transformative Experiences
- Starting a business and failing
- Major illness or recovery
- Significant loss or life change
