# Framework Reference: Scaling and Motivation

## The 0-1-10-100 Scaling Framework

| Stage | Name | Description | Focus Area |
|-------|------|-------------|------------|
| 0-1 | Discovery | Finding the answer from nothing. | Creativity, Innovation |
| 1-10 | Initial Action | Concrete start, early wins, horizontal expansion. | Momentum, Quick Wins |
| 10-100 | Organizational Change | Expanding success to the whole organization. | Scaling, Adoption |
| 100+ | Embedding | Institutionalizing the cycle of evolution. | Culture, DNA |

## Motivation Drivers Comparison

### Achievement
- **Driver:** "Because it is there"
- **Timeframe:** Short-term
- **Requirements:** Clear, specific goals
- **Best For:** Targeted projects with defined endpoints

### Crisis (McKinsey Approach)
- **Driver:** "Boiling frog" / Urgency
- **Timeframe:** Short-term (approx. 3 months)
- **Mechanism:** Creates urgency to force change
- **Risk:** High risk of relapse to comfort once urgency fades
- **Best For:** Turnarounds, critical failures

### Mission (Boston Approach)
- **Driver:** "Why do we exist?"
- **Timeframe:** Long-term / Persistent
- **Mechanism:** Shared purpose
- **Sustainability:** Most sustainable driver
- **Best For:** Cultural transformation, long-term strategic shifts
