# External Forces Strategic Analysis

## When to Use
- Project kickoff requiring understanding of client's external environment
- Strategic planning sessions analyzing competitive positioning
- Identifying external pressures requiring business model adjustments
- Assessing industry changes affecting design, manufacturing, distribution, sales, or service strategies

## Core Workflow

### 1. Identify Four External Forces
Map the competitive environment by analyzing:

- **Suppliers**: Upstream entities providing raw materials or services
- **Customers**: Downstream entities purchasing products or services
- **Competitors**: Market players offering similar products or services
- **Potential Substitutes**: New products or services that could replace the client's value proposition

### 2. Map External Changes
For each of the four forces:
- List specific changes occurring in that domain
- Analyze impacts on the client (positive or negative)
- Identify trends that could disrupt current business models

### 3. Analyze Internal Changes
Identify internal organizational changes affecting the client and their industry:
- Process improvements or degradations
- Capability shifts
- Resource constraints or expansions

### 4. Determine Key Drivers
Identify which factors will force significant adjustments to:
- Product design approaches
- Manufacturing methods
- Distribution channels
- Sales strategies
- Service delivery models

### 5. Form Strategic Perspective
Synthesize findings into:
- Comprehensive competitive environment overview
- Perspectives on how the environment is evolving
- Strategic implications for client positioning

## Key Principles
- **External Focus**: Concentrate on forces outside direct organizational control
- **Actionable Drivers**: Distinguish between background noise and factors requiring strategic response
- **Dynamic View**: Recognize that competitive environments continuously evolve

## Constraints
- Not applicable for purely internal process optimization projects
- Must be combined with internal capability analysis for complete strategic picture

## Output
Strategic assessment of competitive environment with identified key drivers requiring business model adjustments.
