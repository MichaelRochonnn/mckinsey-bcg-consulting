# Matrix Quadrant Decision Strategy

Use this skill when you need to decide how to handle tasks based on your unique skills and their business importance.

## When to Use
- You have multiple tasks and limited capacity
- You're considering whether to delegate or outsource work
- You want to prevent becoming a bottleneck for your team
- You need to decide between doing something yourself vs. leveraging external expertise

## Prerequisites
1. Have identified tasks requiring action
2. Can assess each task's skill level (your unique skill vs. common skill)
3. Can assess each task's importance to business outcomes
4. Have available team members or external partners who can take on tasks

## Constraints
- **DO NOT** apply when you are the only available resource
- **REQUIRES** availability of team members or external partners
- Does not apply to urgent/crisis situations requiring immediate personal action

## Decision Framework

### Step 1: Position the Task in the Matrix

Evaluate the task based on two dimensions:
- **Skill Level**: High (unique to you) or Low (others can do it)
- **Importance**: High (critical impact) or Low (limited impact)

This creates four quadrants:

| Skill \ Importance | Low Importance | High Importance |
|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **High Skill** | Top-Left (Danger Zone) | Top-Right (Sweet Spot) |
| **Low Skill** | Bottom-Left (Ignore) | Bottom-Right (Outsource) |

### Step 2: Apply the Appropriate Strategy

#### Top-Left (High Skill, Low Importance)
**The Danger Zone - Delegate Internally**

**Tendency**: You want to do it because you enjoy using your skills.

**Action**: Endure the urge. Do NOT act.

**Strategy**: Delegate to team members to support their skill development.

**Reasoning**: If you do it yourself, you become a bottleneck. Your team won't grow, and you'll spend time on low-impact work.

---

#### Bottom-Right (Low Skill, High Importance)
**The Critical Gap - Outsource Externally**

**Tendency**: You want to do it because the impact is high.

**Action**: Resolutely entrust to partners with higher expertise.

**Strategy**: Do not ignore it. Learn from external partners to internalize the skill (move it toward Top-Right).

**Reasoning**: Acting with low skills cannot guarantee quality or efficiency. High importance demands expertise.

---

#### Top-Right (High Skill, High Importance)
**Your Zone - Execute Yourself**

This is where you should focus your efforts. Use your unique skills for maximum impact.

---

#### Bottom-Left (Low Skill, Low Importance)
**Ignore or Minimize**

Defer, defer, or eliminate. These tasks distract from high-value work.

### Step 3: Execute the Decision

Based on the quadrant analysis:
- **Delegate (Top-Left)**: Assign to team member with appropriate development needs
- **Outsource (Bottom-Right)**: Engage external partner with proven expertise
- **Execute (Top-Right)**: Prioritize and handle personally
- **Defer/Eliminate (Bottom-Left)**: Remove from immediate focus

## Key Judgment Criteria

Use **Skill Uniqueness** as your benchmark:
- Even if a skill is unique, do not do everything yourself
- Leverage external power to scale your impact
- Focus on activities where your unique skills meet high importance

## Output

Clear decision on whether to:
- Execute personally
- Delegate internally
- Outsource externally
- Defer or eliminate
