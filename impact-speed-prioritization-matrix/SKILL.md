# Impact vs. Speed Prioritization Matrix

## When to Use
Apply this skill when:
- Multiple sub-issues or tasks exist and resources are limited
- You need to determine which initiatives to prioritize
- Strategic planning requires ranking competing initiatives
- You must identify high-impact quick wins versus long-term investments

## Prerequisites
- A list of identified sub-issues or tasks to evaluate
- Understanding of business impact metrics (e.g., bottom-line impact, strategic value)
- Estimates of implementation time or speed to result

## Execution Steps

### 1. Create the Matrix
Construct a 3x3 matrix with:
- **Vertical axis**: Impact (High, Medium, Low)
- **Horizontal axis**: Speed (Fast, Medium, Slow)

### 2. Define Evaluation Criteria
- **Impact**: Magnitude of result, typically defined as "Bottom Line Impact" (e.g., >100 billion yen for large firms, scaled appropriately for organization size)
- **Speed**: Time required to implement or see results

### 3. Plot Each Task
Place each task/sub-issue in the appropriate quadrant based on its impact and speed ratings.

### 4. Apply Zone Strategies

**Zone 1 (High Impact / Fast Speed)**
- The ideal zone
- Prioritize these immediately
- These are your quick wins with maximum value

**Zone 5 (Low Impact / Fast Speed)**
- "Low hanging fruit" zone
- **Warning**: Avoid over-focusing here
- Picking many low-impact tasks does not solve core problems
- Only pursue if they enable Zone 1 or Zone 7 initiatives

**Zone 7 (High Impact / Slow Speed)**
- "White ants" eating the foundation
- Do not ignore these critical long-term initiatives
- These tasks are likely too big or not structured enough

### 5. Convert Zone 7 Tasks
Break down Zone 7 tasks into smaller chunks:
- Identify sub-components that can be executed faster
- Convert large slow initiatives into multiple Zone 1 tasks
- Maintain focus on high impact while improving speed

### 6. Apply the 80/20 Rule
- Focus on the vital 20% of issues that generate 80% of impact
- Discard or defer the trivial 80%
- Prioritize Zone 1 items first, then address converted Zone 7 items

## Output
Generate a ranked list of tasks:
1. Zone 1 tasks (immediate priority)
2. Zone 7 tasks broken down into actionable chunks
3. Other zones only if they support critical initiatives

## Critical Warnings

### Low Hanging Fruit Trap
- Do not accumulate many Zone 5 tasks
- High quantity of fast, low-impact tasks creates false progress
- Ensure Zone 5 tasks directly support strategic objectives

### AI Era Consideration
- In the era of IoT/AI, the "Long Tail" (the 80%) can now be analyzed
- Be careful not to miss "unexpected truths" hidden in the tail
- Use AI tools to re-evaluate discarded tasks for hidden patterns

## Variables
- **Impact**: Metric representing magnitude of result (profit, strategic value, customer satisfaction)
- **Speed**: Metric representing time to implement or see results (days, weeks, months)
